What should you fix first on your website?

Tell us about your business and your website. In a few minutes you'll get a check of four areas and a ranked list of improvements, with the one we'd fix first. New wording and an action plan follow.

Just trying it out?

Load an example business to see the full journey. The first is the real sample site chosen by the project team (homepage source captured 28 Oct 2026); the other three are made up.

About your business

Five quick questions

1. What is the main thing you want website visitors to do?
2. How do most new customers find you?
3. When was your website last updated?
4. Is your website easy to use on a phone?
5. Can customers find your phone number, address or hours within one or two clicks?

What to fix first

Each improvement is labelled High, Medium or Low by how much it gets in the way of your customers and by what you want visitors to do. One is marked Recommended first. If you disagree, pick a different one and the next steps will follow your choice.

Next: wording and an action plan for your priority

Next: Your plan shows a before and after of this priority, wording you can copy where it applies, and the steps to take, each marked as something you can do, DSS can help with, or may need a vendor.
